# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance Lead ensures the delivery meets defined quality standards, release readiness requirements, and user expectations. This role creates confidence that work is validated before and after deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and validation criteria for releases
- Coordinate test planning with Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers
- Track defects, regressions, and remediation follow-up
- Validate that acceptance criteria and user needs are met before release
- Support continuous improvement by identifying recurring quality gaps

### Goals
- Reduce defect escape and rework
- Improve release confidence and customer trust
- Ensure quality is built into delivery rather than added at the end

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and validation reports
- Defect triage and release readiness reviews
- Risk and quality discussions with project and engineering stakeholders

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with Developers to define and validate test coverage and bug fixes
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and user expectations
- Partners with Project Managers to confirm release gates and readiness for deployment
- Coordinates with Release Managers and operational teams to ensure the product is safe to ship

---

## Change Management Lead

### Role Summary
The Change Management Lead helps the organization prepare for and adopt changes introduced by the project. This role focuses on stakeholder readiness, adoption, and communication planning.

### Responsibilities
- Assess change impact for stakeholders, teams, and customers
- Develop communication plans and adoption strategies for new capabilities
- Coordinate updates and readiness activities across groups affected by change
- Support stakeholder education and feedback collection during rollouts
- Work with Product and Project leads to align change timing with release planning

### Goals
- Minimize disruption during change adoption
- Improve stakeholder awareness and readiness
- Increase the likelihood of successful rollout and sustained value realization

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and change impact assessments
- Adoption and readiness communications
- Feedback loops with affected teams and leadership

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers to understand customer and business impacts
- Partners with Project Managers to sequence communications with milestone and release plans
- Coordinates with Developers and Release Managers to ensure support materials match the final implementation
- Helps keep stakeholders informed of decisions and expected impacts across project phases

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Architect guides system design decisions, technical standards, and solution integrity. This role helps ensure the implementation is feasible, scalable, and aligned with the platform strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture patterns, standards, and technical guardrails
- Evaluate technical feasibility and trade-offs for proposed work
- Review designs with engineering leadership and key stakeholders
- Identify dependencies, constraints, and risks that affect delivery
- Support governance for technical decisions and long-term maintainability

### Goals
- Maintain a coherent and sustainable solution design
- Reduce technical debt and avoid design drift
- Support effective implementation decisions across teams

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture decision records
- Technical planning discussions and risk assessment reviews
- Documentation of standards, patterns, and implementation constraints

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on feasibility, cost, and sequencing
- Guides Developers in design alignment and technical trade-offs
- Works with Quality Assurance Lead to ensure architecture supports testability and operational resilience
- Helps Risk Owners identify technical risks and mitigation strategies

---

## Operations / Support Representative

### Role Summary
The Operations and Support Representative brings the day-to-day service perspective into the project. This role helps ensure the solution can be supported, monitored, and operated reliably after go-live.

### Responsibilities
- Review implementation choices for supportability, monitoring, and maintenance needs
- Define operational readiness requirements and support runbooks
- Identify service dependencies and support escalation paths
- Collaborate with teams to reduce operational risk and improve resilience
- Provide feedback on release readiness from an operational perspective

### Goals
- Improve service stability and supportability
- Reduce operational incidents after deployment
- Ensure teams consider long-term sustainability as well as feature delivery

### Typical Communication
- Operational readiness reviews
- Incident and support escalation discussions
- Release and handoff planning meetings

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Reviews requirements with Product Managers to capture operational outcomes and user impact
- Works with Project Managers and Release Managers to align launch readiness and support coverage
- Supports Developers and QA teams with validation of instrumentation, alerts, and recovery steps
- Provides input to Risk Owners and leadership regarding support-related risks

---

## Risk Owner

### Role Summary
The Risk Owner is accountable for specific project risks and for driving the actions needed to reduce or respond to them. This person makes risk status visible and helps ensure issues do not remain unowned.

### Responsibilities
- Identify, assess, and document project risks and dependencies
- Define mitigation plans, owners, and escalation thresholds
- Track issues and ensure response actions are completed on schedule
- Escalate material risks to the appropriate stakeholders or governance body
- Maintain visibility into risk trends across project milestones

### Goals
- Reduce uncertainty and avoid avoidable escalations
- Ensure accountability for risk response actions
- Improve decision quality by surfacing emerging concerns early

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and mitigation dashboards
- Status updates in project forums and governance reviews
- Escalation notes for unresolved or high-impact issues

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Project Managers to monitor risk status and dependency impacts
- Collaborates with Technical Architects and Developers to understand implementation risks
- Works with Product Managers on trade-offs and business impacts related to risk decisions
- Coordinates with Quality Assurance and Operations teams to validate priority and urgency of mitigation work

- Together, these roles improve accountability, communication, and operational clarity across the project lifecycle.
